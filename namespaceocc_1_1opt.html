◆ lindh_model_hessian()

Mat occ::opt::lindh_model_hessian ( const Mat3N & positions,
const IVec & atomic_numbers,
double threshold = 1e-4 )

Lindh's model Hessian (R.

Lindh, A. Bernhardsson, G. Karlstrom and P.-A. Malmqvist, Chem. Phys. Lett. 241, 423 (1995)) in Cartesian coordinates, Hartree/Bohr^2.

Every pair, bend and torsion of atoms contributes k q' q'^T, with q' the coordinate's Cartesian derivative and k = k_r rho_ij, k_f rho_ij rho_jk or k_t rho_ij rho_jk rho_kl, where rho_ij = exp(alpha (r_ref^2 - r_ij^2)) by periodic-table row. Terms whose weight is below threshold are dropped.

positions in Angstrom (3 x N).
