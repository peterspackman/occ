Detailed Description

Translation-rotation internal coordinates (TRIC; L.

-P. Wang and C. Song, J. Chem. Phys. 144, 214108 (2016)): distances, angles, linear bends and dihedrals over the covalent bonds of each fragment, an out-of-plane dihedral at every atom with three bonded neighbours, and the translation and rotation of every fragment, so fragments need no artificial bonds. Cartesian coordinates are added for any atom the rest leave undetermined, so B has rank 3N; the coordinates count as degenerate (to be rebuilt) where that rank drops.
