Detailed Description

A cavity whose points ride rigidly on their owner atoms, for derivatives beyond the frozen-cavity gradient.

With smoothing_width > 0 each area is a0_k · w_k, the smooth weight of solvent::surface::solvent_surface,

w_k = Π_{l ≠ owner(k)} s(|r_k − R_l|), s(d) = ½(1 + erf((d − r_l)/width)),

so areas, and the A diagonal built from them, depend on the geometry; with zero width they are fixed. Derivatives are with respect to the 3N nuclear coordinates, index 3·atom + xyz.
