Detailed Description

Geometry optimizer for minima and first-order saddle points (L.

-P. Wang and C. Song, J. Chem. Phys. 144, 214108 (2016), with the step control of geomeTRIC's current releases).

For a minimum, the step is the Newton step on a positive definite Hessian, shifted (Levenberg) to the length that moves the atoms by the trust radius (RMS, after alignment), in TRIC or Cartesian coordinates; the Hessian starts from Lindh's model and is updated by BFGS. For a saddle point, the step is the restricted-step partitioned RFO step (maximising along the followed mode, minimising along the rest), and the Hessian, best computed, is updated by Bofill's formula. A step whose energy change departs from the model too far is rejected and retaken from the last accepted geometry with a smaller trust radius. Coordinates are rebuilt where they become degenerate, the Hessian carried through Cartesians. Convergence is judged on per-atom Cartesian gradients and aligned displacements.

Use: update() with the energy and gradient at get_next_geometry(), then step(), until step() returns true.
