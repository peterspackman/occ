◆ describe_structures()

std::vector< DescribeOutcome > occ::main::describe_structures ( DescribeConfig const & ,
const std::function< void(const DescribeOutcome &)> & on_outcome = {} )

Describe every structure in the configuration in turn, logging as it goes: each data block of a multi-block CIF is a structure of its own.

A structure that cannot be read is recorded as failed rather than stopping the rest. on_outcome, if given, sees each outcome as soon as it is known.
