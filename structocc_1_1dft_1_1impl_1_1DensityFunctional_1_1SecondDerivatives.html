Detailed Description

First and second derivatives of the energy density (libxc layouts: unpolarized 1 column each; polarized vrho 2, vsigma 3, v2rho2 3, v2rhosigma 6, v2sigma2 6, vtau 2, v2rhotau 4, v2sigmatau 6, v2tau2 3).

LDA leaves the sigma terms empty, LDA and GGA the tau terms.
