Detailed Description

Vectors in the virtual-occupied space (U, B, residuals) are single matrices: nvirt x nocc for a restricted reference; for unrestricted the alpha (nvirt_a x n_alpha) and beta blocks one after the other, column-major, as one column.

Solver::block views either per channel.
