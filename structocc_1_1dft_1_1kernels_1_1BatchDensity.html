Detailed Description

A batch's density matrix: D_s for spin s is rows [s n, (s + 1) n) of D.

When F is set, D_s = F_s F_s^T as well (F_s the rows of F likewise, a few columns: the occupied orbitals), and products X D_s are formed as (X F_s) F_s^T, which costs 2 k / n of the direct product.
