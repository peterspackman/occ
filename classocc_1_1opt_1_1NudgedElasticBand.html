Detailed Description

Nudged elastic band (Henkelman and Jonsson, J.

Chem. Phys. 113, 9978 (2000)) between two structures with the same atoms in the same order, in Cartesian coordinates: the potential force perpendicular to the improved tangent plus the spring force along it (tangents and spring lengths from neighbours superposed on each image, free of rigid rotation), with an optional climbing image (Henkelman, Uberuaga and Jonsson, J. Chem. Phys. 113, 9901 (2000)). The band moves by L-BFGS on the NEB force, its steps capped, without energy checks (the NEB force is not a gradient).

Use: set_endpoint_energies(), then update() with the energies and gradients at images(), then step(), until step() returns true.
