Detailed Description

A solvent held as its converged segment activities, ready to accept any solute at infinite dilution.

The fixed point is solved once for the pure solvent at construction; each solute is then a single test-particle evaluation against it, which is what makes a solvent screen cheap and what lets occ cg reuse one solvent across every surface patch.
